# Tome of Valor - 2D Open-World Turn-Based RPG

Welcome to **Tome of Valor**, a D&D-inspired open-world turn-based adventure built with HTML5, CSS, and Phaser 3!

## Features
- **Retro Visuals at 24fps**: Implements authentic medieval pixel-art styling locked to a cinematic 24 frames per second.
- **Character Customization**: Personalize your hero's Hair Style, Hair Color, and Outfit Color before starting.
- **Grid-Based Travel**: Walk across grasslands, paths, and forests. Stay clear of deep lakes and thick woods.
- **Treasure Chests**: Find treasure boxes around the world to loot gold, heal potions, or the legendary **Magic Sword**!
- **Turn-Based D&D Combat**: Roll initiative! Roll D20s to hit or miss enemies in standard tabletop fashion.
- **Dynamic Weapons & Special Moves**: Loot weapons dropped from monsters (like the *Wizard Wand* or *Iron Greatsword*) and equip them to modify your ATK, combat abilities, and sprite design.
- **Auto Combat Toggle**: Let the AI automatically choose the best combat actions (heal, cast spells, or basic attacks) during battles, while overworld navigation remains manual.
- **Combat Speed Fast Forward**: Speed up delays and animations in combat by 3x using the Fast Forward button.
- **Healing Shrines**: Discover glowing holy shrines that fully heal your HP and Mana.
- **Boss Encounter**: Travel to the Dragon Castle at the top-right of the map to face **Dragon Lord Ashardalon**!

---

## How to Play (No Installation Needed)
Since all pixel art textures are generated dynamically via HTML5 Canvas, **no local server or compilation is required**.

1. Navigate to the game folder: `C:\Users\User\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\open_world_rpg\`
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to open the game directly in any modern browser.

---

## Controls
- **Customization Screen**: Click the left/right arrows (`◀` / `▶`) to modify your hero, then click **`BEGIN QUEST`**.
- **Overworld Map**: Use the **`Arrow Keys`** or **`WASD`** to walk. Traversal is manual.
- **Auto Combat**: Toggle **`AUTO COMBAT: ON/OFF`** in the header. When ON, battles play themselves.
- **Combat Speed**: Toggle **`SPEED: 1x / 3x`** in the header to fast-forward combat rounds.
- **Equip Weapons**: Click on any collected weapon in your **Weapon Arsenal** side panel to equip it.
- **Adventure Log**: Check the right-hand panel to view detailed logs of your dice rolls, damage values, and loot discoveries.
